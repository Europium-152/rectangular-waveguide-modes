# L1 report: merged literature benchmark data (card L1)

Agent L1 (merge), branch `wave-1/L1-literature-data`, 2026-11-08. This supersedes the two independent half-cards
L1a (PR #6) and L1b (PR #5): both raw sets are kept (`benchmarks/literature/raw/a`, `raw/b`,
`docs/literature/raw/a`, `raw/b`, reports `docs/reports/L1a.md`, `L1b.md`) and merged into the final files.

## What was done

* **Merge of the digitized curves** (`benchmarks/literature/scripts/merge_ab.py`, numpy only, regenerates every final
  CSV and `benchmarks/literature/README.md` from the raw sets). Lines: both sets interpolated (y and their reading
  uncertainties) on a common grid inside their own traced segments; final y = mean; uncertainty =
  max(|a − b|/2, max(u_a, u_b)); where only one set covers a point, that set with doubled uncertainty, flagged in the
  new `source` column (`a`, `b`, `ab`). No interpolation across gaps longer than a per-figure limit, nor across gaps
  whose ends both lie within 4 dB of the bottom frame (reflection zeros below the axes; without this rule both sets
  had bridged the zero of Alessandri Fig. 5 d/b = 0.87 at −47/−49 dB). Markers (dots, crosses, circles) are paired
  by mutual nearest neighbour; `x_uncertainty` kept for them. Every header holds source, figure, axes, units,
  geometry, the parameter definition, both digitizers' calibration lines, the merge method, the per-curve a−b
  statistics and every exclusion or curve assignment, with its reason.
* **Every flag investigated on the page images** (staged PDFs, 300–600 dpi renders and native scans, overlays of
  both sets). Resolutions in the table below.
* **Known label doubts settled on the page** (Ma Fig. 11, Reiter Fig. 3(b), 3(d), Fig. 4) and the two Doane
  Fig. 9 questions.
* **Transcriptions D5–D7** compared symbol by symbol between the sets and with the page images; final
  `docs/literature/marcuvitz-steps-and-bends.md`, `docs/literature/doane1989.md`,
  `benchmarks/literature/butterweck1968_numbers.csv`. Marcuvitz formulas evaluated against the book's own curves
  (`benchmarks/literature/scripts/marcuvitz_checks.py`).
* **Spot check**: 3 points per figure re-read on the page (`benchmarks/literature/scripts/spot_check.py`, crops with
  the final value marked were inspected).
* **Test** `tests/test_literature_data.py` (44 cases): every final CSV parses, has the required columns and header
  keys, finite values, positive uncertainties, sorted line grids; the README lists every file; raw sets kept.

### Curve-identity decisions

* **Ma Fig. 11 line styles**: labels printed next to the curves; both sets agree; independently confirmed: c/a=0
  equals Fig. 5's corner converted to |S11| (139 points, rms 0.049 dB, max 0.14 dB); c/a=1.0 equals Fig. 9
  (181 points, rms 0.14 dB, max 0.33 dB); the double-zero curve has |S11| < −30 dB from f/fc 1.555 to 1.95 (39.5 % of
  the band; text "about 39 %" for c/a = 0.631); the zero moves monotonically with c/a (0.60 none, 0.631 at 1.68/1.93,
  0.70 at 1.38).
* **Reiter Fig. 3(b) right branches**: branch to −25.5 dB = γ=105°, to −34.5 dB = γ=120° (the more reflecting curve
  must have the narrower dip below the frame: 14.72–14.85 vs 14.65–15.25 GHz with this pairing). Both sets agree.
* **Reiter Fig. 3(d), 17.2–18 GHz**: not identifiable. Smooth-zero fits below −55 dB favour pairing with γ=135°
  (0.16 dB vs 1.1 dB rms), but single-flank fits predict the returns at 16.77 GHz (γ=105°) and 17.61 GHz (γ=135°),
  neither at 17.22 GHz, and either pairing leaves the other curve's return undrawn. Kept as
  `right branch (gamma=105 or 135)` (L1a had it under γ=105°).
* **Reiter Fig. 4, 11.7 GHz minimum**: l = b. The measured l = 0 line keeps its ripple through both crossings
  (10.35, 11.1 GHz); l = b is smooth from the 9.4 GHz minimum over the 10.6 GHz hump into the 11.7 GHz minimum.
  Minima read by L1 and added as rows (source L1): measured −39.89 dB at 9.41 GHz, measured −60.2 dB at 11.726 GHz,
  theory −54.3 dB at 11.705 GHz; the theory minimum near 9.4 GHz is in a dash gap, below −43.3 dB. The figure has
  tick marks (L1b had calibrated on the frame); the two maps agree to +0.09/+0.14 dB.
* **Doane Fig. 9**: (1) the top frame labelled 2 % lies 174.3 / 173.7 px above the 1.5 % ticks (left/right) while the
  0.5 % tick spacings are 186.5–198 px; the label "2" itself sits at the frame and the shortfall is the same on both
  sides, so it is in the print, not the photograph. Frame kept as 2 %; the difference to a tick-only scale (≤ 0.021 %,
  only the constant curve above 17.87 GHz) added to the uncertainty. (2) The triangular curve rise is real: in the
  photograph the line is 18.5 px above the bottom frame at 15.1 GHz and 59.5 px at 17.9 GHz; merged values 0.049 %
  at 15.025 GHz to 0.149 % at 17.975 GHz (spot check at 17.5 GHz: 0.136 % read, 0.133 ± 0.010 % final).

### Transcriptions (D5, D6, D7)

* **Marcuvitz (D6)**: a normalized LaTeX diff of every equation shows no difference between L1a and L1b in any
  symbol, exponent, index or sign. Resolved: (1) Sec. 5.26a (2a)/(2b), extent of `ln`: the power factor is inside
  (α → 0 limit gives (2c); static values 3.84, 2.47, 1.70, 0.785 at b'/b = 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.5 match Fig. 5.26-3; the
  other reading gives negative B). (2) Ψ(x) = d ln x!/dx = ψ(x+1): reproduces Fig. 5.28-6 (0.645/0.878/1.073 at
  60/90/120°; plain ψ gives 2.88 at 90°). (3) Circuits of Figs. 5.28-2, 5.28-5, 5.29-2 are π networks (shunt jB_a
  across each arm, −jB_b in series): L1a had series jB_a/shunt −jB_b; ABCD check shows only the π network turns into
  the pure shunts of Eqs. (1)–(2) of 5.28a and 5.29a. (4) Elliptic integrals: argument is the modulus k (checked
  against Fig. 5.24-2). (5) **Sec. 5.24 H-plane formulas**: the print has unprimed λ_g in the (2a) denominator (600
  dpi) and `1 + 3α²` in N₂₂ of 5.24b. Evaluated as printed, (2a) matches Fig. 5.24-3 within 1–6 % at a/λ = 0.5 but
  is +8, +25, +72, +402 % off at a/λ = 1.4 for α = 0.3, 0.4, 0.5, 0.6 (pole of X₁₁ at α ≈ 0.63; the pole enters the
  valid range 2a'/λ > 1 from a/λ ≈ 0.7: α = 0.91 at a/λ = 0.7, 0.82 at 1.0). λ_g′ in the denominator makes it worse
  (+86 % at α = 0.5) and does not remove the pole, which sits in X₁₁, X₂₂ (the N terms). For 5.24b the printed
  `1 + 3α²` matches Fig. 5.24-6 better than `3 + α²` at all 7 points (−3 to −17 % vs −4 to −21 %). Neither suspected
  misprint is supported; the cause of the large-a/λ divergence is not identified. Recommendation in the file: use
  the book's figures or (1c)/(2c) near α → 1, not (1a)–(3a), for a/λ ≳ 0.7.
* **Doane (D7)**: identical symbol by symbol (eqs. 14–18, ΔβL_B, |ΔβL_t|), confirmed on the page; only typography
  (a multiplication dot) differs. Merged file keeps L1a's LaTeX and adds L1b's worked-example arithmetic
  (5.70 in and 84.1 in at 17.7 GHz vs the paper's 5" and 70").
* **Butterweck (D5)**: every item present in both has the same value, page and sentence; items only in L1a (B11,
  P1, P9, conversion clause of P4) checked on the pages. Added checks: eq. (21) reproduces 3.87 and 35.95 dB/m
  (paper 4 and 36) and 0.58/5.39 dB over 15 cm (paper 0.6/5.4); eq. (16) 56 Ω; eq. (5) 1.47 dB/cm (paper 1.5).

## Open issues

1. Marcuvitz Sec. 5.24a (1a)–(3a) are unusable for a/λ ≳ 0.7 at moderate α (pole of X₁₁); misprint not
   identified. WP4 should validate against mode matching/COMSOL, or the book's figures.
2. Reiter Fig. 3(d) right branch (17.2–18 GHz) cannot be assigned to γ = 105° or 135°.
3. Doane Fig. 9 top-frame anomaly is in the print; the frame is taken as 2 % with the alternative added to the
   uncertainty. Only the constant curve above 17.87 GHz is affected.
4. D8 (Kotik & Sivov 1965) not done: scan not available.
5. Uncertainties on near-vertical flanks at reflection zeros are large (up to about 13 dB); the zeros themselves are
   not in the data (below the frames).
6. The raw digitizer scripts contain scratch paths and need the PDFs; `merge_ab.py` and `marcuvitz_checks.py` run
   without them, `spot_check.py` needs them.
7. `benchmarks/registry.json` (WP7) does not list these files yet.

## Conventions issues

* CONVENTIONS.md §9 asks for an `uncertainty` column; the merged files add `x_uncertainty` (markers) and `source`.
* Geometry parameters differ between sources and from §6: Alessandri's d and Reiter's a_opt/b_opt are measured from
  the inner corner perpendicular to the miter; Ma's c from the removed outer corner along the outer walls; Reiter's
  γ is the interior angle (deflection = 180° − γ); Marcuvitz's θ is the deflection angle. Magnitudes do not depend on
  reference planes, phases do. Definitions are in every header and in the README.
