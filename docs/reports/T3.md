## Results

* **Exact equations, linear in θ′.** In co-rotating coordinates the twist is a straight guide filled with
  ε₀G, μ₀G (G = inverse metric). The θ′² terms cancel exactly, and θ″ never appears.
* **The card's generator is correct, but its matrix element does not enter as the card implies.**
  C_pq = ∬ e_p·𝒢e_q is not antisymmetric on a rectangle. Its symmetric part is a wall integral (surface charge ×
  normal wall velocity). When the boundary terms are kept: **dV/dz = −iβI/Y + θ′CᵀV,
  dI/dz = −iβYV − θ′CI**, i.e. K±_pq = ½(r_pq C_qp ∓ r_qp C_pq). This form is lossless and reciprocal for any θ′(z).
  The naive form (−θ′C in both equations) is neither. The Galerkin check (Maxwell's equations, no modal series)
  confirms T3: errors 4e-3 k at 598 modes, extrapolating to < 6e-4 k. The naive form and the
  antisymmetric-only form converge to values off by 1–6 % of k. For TE₁₀–TE₀₁ the symmetric part is zero, so
  Katsenelenbaum's and Doane's two-mode results are unaffected.
* **Closed forms** (box T3-2) for TE–TE, TE–TM, TM–TE (identically 0) and TM–TM. They match quadrature to
  **9.0e-15** relative (target 1e-10) on 5 guides with 18–36 modes. Selection rule: m+m′ and n+n′ both odd, so a
  twist always changes the family.
* **c = 8/π² = 0.810569469138702 for TE₀₁←TE₁₀, independent of the aspect ratio**: confirmed (closed form exact;
  quadrature 6e-15 for a/b = 0.1 … 10).
* **Square guide**: two-mode truncation gives exactly a₁₀ = cos cθ, a₀₁ = −sin cθ for any profile. The
  polarization rotates by **−cθ in the local frame**, i.e. (1 − 8/π²)θ = 0.189θ in the fixed frame. The field does
  not follow the guide. Confirmed (1e-12), and it agrees with Katsenelenbaum eq. 4.23. Full basis, uniform twist:
  c_eff/c − 1 ≈ 0.8(τ/k)² (8e-5 at τ/k = 0.01).
* **End frames** (item 4 rewritten to the coordinator's PR #7 rule): the co-rotating frame is the guide frame, the
  GSM has the same basis at both ports, no FrameChange. Z(−90°)Z(+90°) = I to 1.1e-15. The twist commutes with the
  H↔E frame change (1.8e-16). Turning a twist around keeps the sign of θ and reverses the profile (3.6e-15).
* **Eq. 18** transcribed from the scan: P_c ≃ (8cθ)²/(ΔβL_t)⁴. It is Unger 1957 eq. 20 applied to the triangle.
  Values at 15 / 16.5 / 18 GHz: 0.302 / 0.465 / 0.684 %; first order TE₁₀→TE₀₁ triangular 0.0054 / 0.0001 / 0.0452 %
  (the reviewer's values reproduced).

## Open issues

1. **Fig. 9, triangular twist.** The full multimode solution of the T3 equations gives 0.114 / 0.247 / 0.603 %
   total spurious power at 15 / 16.5 / 18 GHz, against Fig. 9's ≈ 0.05–0.08 %. The excess is second-order
   conversion to TE₁₂, TE₀₃ and TM₁₂, the modes Doane & Anderson name as "important" in tall guide. The constant
   and cosine curves are reproduced (cosine 0.352 / 0.867 / 0.943 % vs ≈ 0.35 / 0.85 / 1.03 %). The exception is
   the constant twist at 16.5 GHz: 0.70 % here, of which 0.61 % is TE₂₁, vs ≈ 0.03 % in Fig. 9. Fig. 9 looks like a
   calculation with few modes (TE₁₀+TE₀₁ alone gives 1.21 / 0.007 / 1.55 % constant and 0.32 / 0.87 / 1.04 %
   cosine). Suggested COMSOL case for wave 2: WR187 tall, 20-inch 90° triangular twist, 16.5 and 18 GHz.
2. **The card's statement that eq. 18 "behaves as an envelope" is not true in general.** The exact first-order
   triangle is 4 sin⁴(ΔβL/4) × eq. 18: its envelope is 4× eq. 18 and its phase average 1.5×. It lies below eq. 18 at
   the three frequencies only because 4 sin⁴ < 1 there.
3. **The coupled-mode series converges algebraically** (≈ 1/cutoff) for strong twists. This is a consequence of
   the wall-normal H component that no mode function has. It is negligible for practical rates (corrections
   ∝ τ²), but WP6 should not expect spectral convergence.
4. **A zero-length twist is not a physical frame change** in a truncated basis: exp((π/2)M₁) on a square guide
   gives a TE₁₀/TE₀₁ block of ≈ [[0.026, 1.018], [−1.018, 0.026]] at 396 modes, not [[0, 1], [−1, 0]].
   L → 0 and N → ∞ do not commute. The card's check is the algebraic one (Z(−θ)Z(θ) = I), which holds exactly.
5. Katsenelenbaum eq. 4.29 has a typo (4θ(L−z)L² should be 4θ(L−z)/L²).

## Conventions issues

* CONVENTIONS §2 (before PR #7) said a twist ends in a rotated frame and is followed by a FrameChange. Item 4 is
  written to the PR #7 rule instead. With that rule the card's old check ("+90° twist, frame change, −90° twist
  compose to identity") needs two frame changes or none; with none it is Z(−90°)Z(+90°) = I, which is what is
  checked.
* Not an issue, but worth stating in CONVENTIONS when WP6 lands: the sign of θ is counterclockwise about +z (the
  propagation direction from port 1 to port 2). It is the same in the H and E plane frames, and it is kept when
  a twist is turned around.
